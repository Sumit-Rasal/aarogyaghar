About AarogyaGhar

A non-profit organization serving neglected senior citizens with free healthcare coordination and financial support — because every elder deserves dignity and care.

Our Story

Founded in 2026, AarogyaGhar emerged from witnessing a painful reality: many elderly individuals in our community suffer not because healthcare doesn't exist, but because they have no one to help them access it.

We saw grandparents abandoned by their children, widows living alone with no support, and elders skipping life-saving medication because they couldn't afford it. These weren't strangers — they were our neighbors, our community members.

We knew we had to act. Today, AarogyaGhar stands as a pillar of hope, bringing together volunteers, donors, and healthcare providers to serve those who once cared for us — now when they need us the most.

Our Mission

To ensure that no senior citizen — especially those abandoned, neglected, or financially struggling — is denied quality healthcare. We provide free medical coordination, hospital accompaniment, and financial grants for treatment. Our mission is seva (selfless service) without expecting anything in return.

Our Vision

A society where aging is embraced with dignity, where no elderly person feels unwanted or alone, and where community comes together to support those who once built it. We dream of a world where every grandparent receives the love and care they deserve.

Core Values

These principles guide every action we take in serving our elders.

Compassion

Treating every elder with the kindness, love, and respect they deserve.

Equality

Serving all elders equally, regardless of caste, religion, or background.

Integrity

Complete transparency in our donations, spending, and activities.

Community

Bringing volunteers, donors, and families together to serve.

Selfless Service

Seva without expectation — giving because it's right, not for reward.
