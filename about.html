About AarogyaGhar

Compassionate healthcare coordination for seniors, built on trust, care, and human connection.

Our Mission

AarogyaGhar exists to simplify healthcare for seniors and families. We act as a single point of coordination for medical care, home services, and emergency support — ensuring dignity, comfort, and peace of mind.

Why We Started

Managing healthcare for aging parents is complex and stressful. AarogyaGhar was founded to remove this burden by combining technology, medical expertise, and empathy into one seamless service.

What Makes Us Different

  • Dedicated care coordinators
  • Trusted hospital & doctor network
  • Home-first healthcare approach
  • 24/7 support for families
